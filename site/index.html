Your numbers, loud and clear.

LozCharts is my charting library for the web. 26 chart types, from the good old line chart to the flow diagram, and you can read every one with a keyboard and a screen reader. Move your pointer over the bars and find the month this store beat its goal.

02Try it

Play with your data.

Pick a type, change the data, turn on the lights. Next to it is the code that draws the chart, and whatever just changed lights up. Copy it and take it to your page.

Whatever lights up just changed. When you copy, you get every row, not just the four on screen.

03All 26 types

26 ways to tell one story.

Choose by the question you want to answer, not by the name of the chart. Hover over one and it draws itself again. None of them is a picture: they’re the same charts you’ll put on your page, with their tooltip and keyboard support.

04Accessible

Makes sense without seeing it.

What you hear on a screen reader

Active users by city, in thousands.

Click the chart and move around with the arrow keys. Up above, word for word, is what someone using a screen reader hears.

  • You get around with the arrow keys, Home, End, and Escape.
  • Each line has its name at the end, so nobody has to guess colors.
  • The palette is tested for color blindness. Use the button and compare.
  • Under every chart there’s a table that only screen readers read.

05Lightweight

79 KB. That’s it.

Compressed, with nothing else to install or update every Monday. Save the chart as an image (PNG or SVG) and the data as a spreadsheet (CSV). It has zoom, and it switches to dark mode on its own when your system does.

It comes with an llms.txt, a cheat sheet so the AI you code with knows which options exist and doesn’t make up new ones.

Works with what you already use

React. Angular. Vue. Svelte. Django + HTMX.
